Install

  1. Tap Download, then open the file.
  2. If Android asks, allow your browser to install unknown apps.
  3. Tap Install, open Kinn and create your account.

Updates

Kinn tells you when a new version is out (Me › Update available). Updates install over the app and keep your data.
Your data is end-to-end encrypted. Save your recovery key when the app shows it: without it, nobody (not even us) can restore your account if you forget your password.
No Android phone? Use Kinn in your browser.
