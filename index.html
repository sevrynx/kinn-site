Feelings, not just checkmarks

Tick a todo or a habit and say how it felt. Do a quick mood check-in whenever you like.

Expected vs. felt

Guess how something will feel before you do it. Kinn shows you the nice surprises and the tough ones.

What lifts and drains you

An emotion calendar, your mood by weekday, and the things that leave you better or worse off.

Habits and goals

Streaks, a weekly feeling strip for every habit, and goals that bring your todos and habits together.

Works offline

Everything saves on your phone first and syncs when you're back online. Tick things right on the home-screen widgets.

Private by design

Your entries are encrypted on your device before they leave it. We can't read them, and nobody else can either.

Your feelings stay yours

  • End-to-end encrypted. The server only ever stores scrambled data. It can't see your goals, todos, habits or how you felt.
  • Your password never leaves your device. It is turned into keys on your phone or in your browser.
  • One recovery key. You get it when you register. Keep it safe: without your password or that key, nobody can restore your account, not even us.
